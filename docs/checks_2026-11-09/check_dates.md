## 1. High-income planning area lists, 2019-2026

What happens each year: Planning staff work out the list, the Planning Board votes (from 2024 on) to send it to the County Council, and a transmittal letter follows. No source gives an "effective date" separate from the Board meeting. Chapter 25A only says the Board "must update the Planning Area designations ... at least annually" (Bill 38-17, Sec. 25A-5(d), lines 58-59). The practice described in the sources is a January update: "maps will be updated each year by January 1" (2018); "updates the requirements by January every year" (2024 letter).

## 2. Effective date of the 15% high-income requirement and grandfathering

The high-income 15% rule comes from **Bill 38-17** (Riemer), not Bill 34-17. Bill 34-17 (Floreen) is the general MPDU rewrite. It allows a master-plan base of up to 15% and uses "high-income planning areas" only for alternative payments and locations. Both bills were enacted 2018-07-24 and took effect 2018-10-31, with the same grandfathering clause.

## 4. 12500 Ardennes (Site Plan 820240060, Twinbrook): why the minimum is 12.5%

**Conclusion: this is grandfathering, not a geography exclusion.** The project's sketch plan (320180200) was accepted on 2018-06-08, before 2018-10-31. Bill 38-17 Sec. 2 excludes "an applicant who has submitted a sketch plan that the Planning Board has accepted as complete before October 31, 2018". That applicant and its later applications are reviewed under Chapter 25A as of 2018-10-30, which had no high-income 15% rule. No source excludes Twinbrook or any part of North Bethesda. The GIS polygons agree: North Bethesda in high_income_planning_areas_2019, _2023 and _2024 is identical to the planning_areas.geojson polygon (symmetric difference 0.0 acres; 5,917 acres in each).

## 5. Other master/sector plans and 15% MPDUs

Planning describes earlier plans as using 15% as a "priority public amenity". Quote: "Before the county code (Chapter 25A) changed in 2018 to allow a master-planned requirement of 15%, some sector and master plans prioritized 15% MPDUs as the highest priority public amenity for residential development." ## Where the sources disagree with the script

1. **REQUIREMENT_START = 2018-10-01 is wrong.** Both bills took effect **2018-10-31** and apply to applications "accepted as complete on or after" that date. Applications accepted 2018-10-01 to 2018-10-30 should get the old rules (12.5%).
2. **Sketch-plan grandfathering is not modeled.** Bill 38-17 Sec. 2 excludes any applicant whose **sketch plan was accepted as complete before 2018-10-31**, and their later applications. 12500 Ardennes (820240060; sketch plan 320180200 accepted 2018-06-08) is one case: the script gives it 0.15, the resolution uses 12.5%. Other site plans that follow pre-Oct-2018 sketch plans (or preliminary plans accepted before that date) will have the same issue. Using the date of the earliest related application (sketch or preliminary plan) would fix this.
3. **Oct 31, 2018 until the first list is not "no designation".** Planning published a 7-area map in Sept 2018 as in force from 2018-10-31: Goshen, Lower Seneca, Darnestown, Travilah, Potomac, North Bethesda, Bethesda-Chevy Chase. It is the GIS 2019 list minus Upper Rock Creek. No Board vote on it was found.
4. **Assumed Jan 31 adoption, 2019-2022: no Board action found.** The only stated timing is "the maps will be updated each year by January 1" (2018). The 2024 letter says the practice is "the Planning Board updates the requirements by January every year". January 1 is better supported than January 31, but neither is documented for 2019-2022.
5. **2023 date: 2023-01-12** (Board briefing, "Received briefing", no vote), not 2023-01-31.
6. **2026 date: 2026-03-12** (Board vote 5-0), not 2026-01-31. Acceptances from 2026-01-31 to 2026-03-11 are currently put on the 2026 list. Under the sources they belong on the 2025 list, which differs: Kemp Mill/4 Corners and Poolesville in, Dickerson out.
7. **2024 (2024-01-18) and 2025 (2025-01-23) confirmed.**
8. **SSDAC 2022-10-25 matches no SSDAC event.** Plan approved 2022-05-26; M-NCPPC adopted 2022-06-15; SMA H-146 and the DSS Overlay ZTA 22-03 (Ord. 19-38) both 2022-10-18; the overlay took effect 20 days later, about **2022-11-07**. The one Oct 25 reference in the Council minutes belongs to a different ZTA.
9. **Great Seneca can be applied.** It requires 15% (2024 plan; Council Res. 20-598, **2024-07-30**; M-NCPPC adopted 2024-09-18). The plan's own wording ("aligned with current county policy") is softer than SSDAC's.
10. **Forest Glen Metro Station parking lot (2020, Res. 19-406, 2020-03-31)** is a site-specific 15% that Planning recognizes. It is not in the GIS layer. Westbard (2016) and Chevy Chase Lake Site 5 (2013) also have plan text using "require"/"must" for 15% (or the Site 5 public-benefit package), but Planning does not list them as 15% plans. They are flagged here and not resolved.
11. **Bill number.** The script docstring says Bill 38-17, which is correct for the high-income rule. The brief's "Bill 34-17" is the companion bill (master-plan base and alternative compliance). Both share the same effective date and grandfathering.
